Our Story

Grown from a passion for the perfect cup

Ember & Grain began as a single espresso cart on Maple Street — just a hand-grinder, a dream, and an embarrassingly overpowered kettle. Today we're a full kitchen and café, but the obsession with sourcing and craft has never wavered.

Every bean we roast is traceable to its farm. Every dish on our menu changes with the season. We believe great coffee and honest food are acts of hospitality — and we take both seriously.

  • ✦
    Direct Trade

    Relationships with farmers, not brokers.

  • ✦
    Seasonal Kitchen

    The menu follows the harvest, always.

  • ✦
    Zero-Waste Roasting

    Chaff composted, bags reused, waste minimal.
